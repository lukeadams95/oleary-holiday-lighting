Roofline is what we do most.

It's the line people see from the street, and it's the one that goes wrong on a homemade job — sagging strands, dark gaps at the corners, an extension cord running down the front of the house. We measure your roof and build the run to fit it.

How we install

  • Every strand is cut to your measurements. No leftover length looped over the gutter, no gaps where a strand ran short.
  • Clips are matched to your shingles and gutters. Nothing is stapled, nailed, or screwed into the house.
  • Cords are run out of sight and tied into one timer so the whole house comes on and off together.
  • We work on steep pitches and second and third stories. Firefighter owned — ladders and roofs are the part we're least worried about.

Bulb options

Commercial-grade C9 bulbs, the size most people picture on a roofline. Available in 10 colors — orange, yellow, red, cool white, warm white, royal blue, teal, purple, pink, and green. We also carry mini lights in all the same colors for trees and bushes.

We'll bring samples to the quote so you can see them against your brick or siding before you decide.

What's included

  • Free on-site design and quote.
  • All lights, clips, cords, and timers.
  • Professional install on your chosen date.
  • Season-long maintenance — if a strand goes out or a clip lets go, we come fix it, usually next day.
  • Takedown in January. Storage in our warehouse until next year.

One price covers the whole season. No per-visit charge, no storage fee.

Common questions

Do I need to buy the lights?

No. Everything is ours and stays ours. That's what makes the maintenance and storage work.

What if something goes out in December?

Call us. We come fix it, usually next day, at no extra cost.

Will the clips damage my roof or gutters?

No. They're sized to your shingles and gutters and come off clean in January. Nothing gets stapled or nailed.

When should I book?

ASAP. We fill up fast, and as spots on the schedule run out, price tends to increase.

Do you do commercial?

Yes — we've done a number of commercial properties, including businesses and stores, HOAs, and a few city lighting projects.
