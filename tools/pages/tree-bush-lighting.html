Common questions

How big a tree can you do?

The tallest trees we've done are around 45 ft. Anything much taller than that needs a lift, which increases the price.

Will wrapping hurt the tree?

No. Strands come off in January and nothing is fastened into the bark.

Can I add trees to a roofline quote?

Yes. Most people do it at the on-site visit once they see the layout.

Do you light trees without a roofline install?

Yes — same as with wreaths. It costs a bit more than it would as an add-on to a roofline install.
